# HealthBar

## Product Purpose
Gamified nutrition and weight-loss tracking for iOS. Logging meals, hitting macro goals, and keeping streaks earns XP, levels, ranks (Iron to Diamond), daily quests, and badges. The RPG loop makes adherence feel like progression instead of punishment. A friend system adds co-op pressure: a username directory, friend requests, a weekly-adherence leaderboard, and read-only friend profiles.

## Users
Individuals working on weight loss or body recomposition who respond to game mechanics: streaks, ranks, visible progress. They log food several times a day on their phone, often quickly between activities. Friends are training partners and accountability buddies, not strangers.

## Brand & Tone
Two switchable skins that both ship first-class:
- **RPG (pixel)**: Silkscreen pixel font, time-of-day color themes, wooden tab bar, parchment cards, gold treasure accents. Cozy retro game, not harsh arcade.
- **Clean**: SF Rounded, soft emerald palette (light and dark), minimal rounded cards.

Tone is encouraging and playful, never shaming. Core design law from the project: XP rewards healthy behavior, not perfection; streaks are protected (no harsh resets); ranks are identity signals, not skill gates. Friend comparisons celebrate the leader; they never humiliate the bottom.

## Anti-references
- MyFitnessPal-style sterile data tables.
- Generic SaaS dashboard cards (icon + heading + text repeated).
- The purple "AI gradient" aesthetic.
- Leaderboards that shame (red, "last place", down arrows).

## Strategic principles
- Game feel first: numbers should feel like loot, ranks like trophies.
- Both skins get equal care; every component renders through adaptive modifiers (AppFont, adaptiveCard) so no feature is clean-only.
- Detail lives in profiles; lists stay scannable.
- Delight is reserved for moments (rank-ups, badges, podium), not spread over every surface.
